Business Context

Problem Statement & Industry Challenge

Why traditional sales tracking fails modern executive teams.

⚠️ Traditional Reporting Pitfalls

  • Gross Sales Illusion: Companies focus only on total sales without factoring in COGS and discounts, masking unprofitable orders.
  • Reporting Latency: Manual monthly Excel preparation takes 10–15 hours, resulting in stale business decisions.
  • Territorial Blindspots: Inability to compare territory sales with regional profit margins in real-time.
  • Inflexible Static Views: Lack of drill-downs for date ranges, specific categories, or product segments.

💡 The Proposed Analytics Solution

  • Automated Data Pipeline: Cleans, deduplicates, and engineers temporal features in seconds.
  • True Margin Transparency: Tracks unit costs, tiered discounts, and exact net profit percentages.
  • Interactive Self-Service: Instant web-based filtering across dates, territories, and product catalogs.
  • Multi-Channel Delivery: Streamlit web dashboard, Power BI visuals, and 7-tab Excel workbooks.

Data Engineering

Data Cleaning & Feature Engineering

Transforming raw transaction logs into an analytics-ready model.

🛠️ Data Cleaning Steps

  • Deduplication: Automated hash check on Order ID to eliminate duplicates.
  • Type Casting: Currency strings parsed to float64; dates to datetime64.
  • Missing Value Treatment: Filled numerical nulls with column median; categorical with mode.
  • Outlier Validation: Confirmed positive unit prices and non-negative unit quantities.

⚙️ Engineered Business Features

  • Temporal Dimensions: Year, Month, Year_Month, Quarter, Day_of_Week.
  • Profit Margin %: (Profit / Sales) * 100 calculated per transaction.
  • Average Order Value (AOV): Total Revenue / Distinct Order Count.
  • Volume Tier Flag: Standard (1–4), Bulk (5–9), Enterprise Wholesale (10+).
